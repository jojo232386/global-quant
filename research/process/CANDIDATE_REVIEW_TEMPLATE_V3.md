# GMAQ Candidate Review Template v3

Status: `CANDIDATE_ONLY_NO_EXPL_ID`

This card is completed before a hypothesis review can consider an `EXPL-*`
identity. It is not a preregistration, data admission, gold sample,
implementation, backtest, formal run, or approval.

- **HYPOTHESIS_ID:** provisional candidate identifier only.
- **MECHANISM:** one falsifiable economic mechanism, not a formula or parameter
  variation.
- **WHY_EXISTS:** observable causal story and evidence that would contradict it.
- **DIFFERENCE_FROM_FAILED_WORK:** mechanism-level comparison with the Factor
  Graveyard and all relevant completed/rejected work.
- **REQUIRED_DATA:** exact fields, source, historical coverage, and identity
  mapping required before admission.
- **PIT_REQUIREMENTS:** publication/availability timestamps, decision and
  execution clocks, revisions, lifecycle/listing treatment, and forward-return
  endpoint rules.
- **EXPECTED_FAILURE:** predeclared ways the mechanism, data contract, or
  economic value may fail.

## Candidate review matrix

Record `YES`, `NO`, or `UNVERIFIED` with evidence for each:

- Mechanism independence from failed work
- Existing-data availability
- PIT risk
- Research cost and required infrastructure
- Recommended next pre-experiment stage: `HYPOTHESIS_REVIEW`, `DATA_ADMISSION`,
  or `STOP`

No parameter, window, cost, split, or formula variation may use this template
to masquerade as a new Alpha. A completed review still does not authorize live
trading.
