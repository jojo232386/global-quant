# GMAQ Vendor Proof-of-Fit Template

Status: `TEMPLATE_FAIL_CLOSED_NO_PURCHASE_AUTHORIZATION`

Use only for Route B feasibility after a mechanism has demonstrated value and
Data Admission has identified a precise blocker. This template is not a vendor
contact, purchase request, budget approval, data admission, or live-trading
authorization.

- **CANDIDATE_ID:** `UNASSIGNED`
- **MECHANISM_VALUE_EVIDENCE:** `UNVERIFIED`
- **EXACT_DATA_BLOCKER:** `UNVERIFIED`
- **VENDOR / PRODUCT / VERSION:** `UNVERIFIED`
- **REQUIRED_FIELDS:** `UNVERIFIED`
- **COVERAGE:** instruments, venues, start/end dates, and known gaps — `UNVERIFIED`
- **TIMESTAMPS:** event, publication, availability, decision, and execution clocks — `UNVERIFIED`
- **PIT_AND_AVAILABILITY_SEMANTICS:** as-of behavior and whether historical
  values were knowable at each decision time — `UNVERIFIED`
- **REVISIONS_AND_BACKFILL_POLICY:** versioning, correction history, and
  restatement handling — `UNVERIFIED`
- **COST:** currency, license scope, recurring/one-off fees, and total
  expected cost — `UNVERIFIED`
- **EXPECTED_BLOCKER_REMOVED:** exact Tier 2 field the vendor evidence would
  establish, or `NONE`
- **EVIDENCE:** dated documentation, sample/schema, and explicit limitations — `UNVERIFIED`
- **DECISION:** `NOT_APPROVED`; permitted values after review are
  `NOT_APPROVED`, `FEASIBLE_PENDING_HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION`, or `NOT_FIT`

No purchase may be inferred from `FEASIBLE_PENDING_HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION`.
Purchase requires a separate, explicit human authorization.
